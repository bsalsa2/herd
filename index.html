Open Herd How it works

Runs in your browser. No account. Your records stay on your device.

24.8°Living room
  • Baseline

    Compared with itself

    Each entry is measured against that animal’s own last 14 days. A 30% drop in energy or activity, or low intake, is flagged with the exact reason.

  • Climate

    Surroundings that suit the animal

    Group animals into spaces and check temperature, humidity and air against what each species and individual needs. Add sensors whenever you’re ready.

  • Yours

    Private and portable

    Everything is stored in your browser. Export a JSON backup any time and import it anywhere.
